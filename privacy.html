Privacy

This draft explains the intended approach and will be reviewed before launch.

Information we may receive

The site currently stores cart quantity in your browser so your cart can persist. When checkout is connected, the payment provider may collect information needed to process payment, shipping, receipts, fraud prevention, and customer support.

How information is used

Information will be used to process orders, communicate about purchases, provide support, prevent fraud, and improve the storefront. We do not intend to sell customer information.

Third-party services

The storefront uses hosted services such as GitHub Pages and Google Fonts. Stripe or another payment provider will be identified here when live checkout is selected.

Updates

This policy will be updated with the final business contact, service providers, retention practices, and customer choices before launch.
