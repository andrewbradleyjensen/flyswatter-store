FLYSWATTER — THE BOARD GAME

Swat the flies.
Save the food.

Flyswatter is a fast, chaotic tabletop game where everyone races to swat flying tiles, steal food, and build a matching bench before someone calls, “PARTY’S OVER!”

$29
AGES5+
PLAYERS2–5
PLAY STYLEFast-action tabletop

What’s included

  • 5 flyswatters
  • 5 picnic benches
  • 1 cooler (game bag)
  • 1 swatting zone mat
  • 42 food tiles: 6 hotdogs, 6 hamburgers, 6 sodas, 6 watermelons, 6 chips, 6 ice cream bars, and 6 ants
  • 1 horsefly (black marble)

How to play

01 · SWAT

Flip a tile onto the picnic blanket. Hit the fly before anyone else.

02 · STEAL

Claim flies and replace tiles on your bench.

03 · SCORE

Match foods, manage ants, and avoid the horsefly.
